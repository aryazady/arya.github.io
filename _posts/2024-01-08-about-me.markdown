I started studying at the Azad University of Iran in software engineering. While studying, I began to work as a developer, which provided a great opportunity for me to develop advanced skills and gain a deeper understanding of how different programming languages work.

During the last two semesters of my education, I decided to continue my education in a related major. I was accepted into Bioinformatics at Iran's top-ranked university, Sharif University of Technology, by placing 36th in the nationwide university entrance exam. After reviewing various topics, I found the COBRA field in Systems Biology more appealing than the others and focused my research on it. Additionally, I had the chance to become acquainted with various fields in computer science by taking courses such as Reinforcement Learning, Natural Language Processing, and Medical Image Processing, which eventually helped me to have broader ideas in Systems Biology.

# Education
I studied for my BS for 5.5 years at Azad University and my MS for 3 years at Sharif University of Technology.

![Sharif University of Technology]({{site.baseurl}}/assets/img/sharif-university.jpg){:style="display:block; margin-left:auto; margin-right:auto"}

In Sharif, I have learned many important aspects of Computer Science. The courses were perfectly organized to review key concepts and require knowledge of new findings. Most courses required graduate students to present one or two decent recently published papers, which at first seemed irritating, reading recent papers in various fields that you were not even familiar with. However, it helped us become zero-shot learners who could extract key information from each paper in an unfamiliar field. Furthermore, the courses had plenty of assignments to improve our hands-on code experience. Projects varied from course to course, some professors made it mandatory, and some introduced a bonus project.

Overall, I gained most of my knowledge by attending classes, completing assignments, presenting papers and finally working on my thesis for the seminar at Sharif University. All of this helped me to develop the necessary proficiencies to research and properly implement my ideas.

# Work Experience
It was during the third semester of my undergraduate program that I discovered my passion for application development. Motivated to refine my coding skills and prepare for my future career, I sought a position aligned with my field of study. This led me to begin my journey as an Android development intern. Although the startup I joined disbanded after six months, the experience was invaluable, offering me hands-on exposure to real-world development.

With newfound time on my hands, I focused on expanding my skill set, searching for new programming languages and frameworks. Soon after, I secured a position as a senior Android developer at a moving company. While the role further improved my technical expertise, I realized that a career solely centered around coding wasn’t my ultimate goal. This realization drove me to pursue higher education, where I could combine my technical skills with research—a path I truly desired.
Working for startups and small companies provided me with a unique blend of technical and communication skills. I gained a deep understanding of programming concepts while learning to adapt to the ever-changing demands of the industry. These experiences shaped my ability to quickly acquire and apply knowledge in diverse programming environments.
